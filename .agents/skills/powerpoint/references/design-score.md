# Internal design score

Before delivery, rate the deck from 1–5 on readability, hierarchy, coherence, variety, image quality, rhythm, density, alignment, and contrast. Inspect the rendered slides, not only source code. A score is diagnostic, not user-facing by default.

- Any category at 1 requires correction.
- Readability, hierarchy, alignment, or contrast below 4 requires correction unless constrained by an authoritative supplied template.
- Coherence must score at least as high as variety.
- A high variety score does not compensate for inconsistent typography, palette, or imagery.

Record brief evidence by slide for weak categories. Apply only simple, safe fixes during automatic review and keep the total build–render–inspect correction cycle to three passes.
